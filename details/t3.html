Communication (C7CE3)

Level 3 competency

Justification

Given our diverse team (three nationalities), we used English as our primary language for communication to ensure clarity and inclusivity. Additionally, due to the group size (seven people), we held a short daily meeting to track progress and keep everyone aligned with their tasks.

Midway through the project, we gave an oral presentation to share our progress with other students and professors. Everyone contributed to preparing the presentation slides, and I was one of the team members who presented our work.

The presentation lasted around ten minutes and was delivered in front of four professors and several students working on other projects. I spoke for approximately three minutes, presenting the image processing work I had conducted during the first week of the project. This included explaining the basic simulation framework, the use of PSFs from Zemax, and the application of Wiener filtering for image recovery.

As part of our group coordination, we agreed to share our individual DISC folders through a common OwnCloud directory. A link to this shared folder is provided below.
